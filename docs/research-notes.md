# MapleBenefits — Research Notes (verified 2026-09-01)

All figures verified against official Government of Canada / Government of BC
pages and reputable secondary sources on 2026-09-01. Each benefit's data file
carries `lastUpdated: "2026-09-01"` and its official source URL.

## Federal

- **DTC** — 2025 federal disability amount $10,138; federal credit ~$1,448/yr;
  BC provincial portion ~$491/yr → combined ~$1,900/yr tax reduction. Child
  supplement $5,914 (2025). Needs Form T2201 certified by a medical
  practitioner. Gateway to CDB, RDSP, CWB disability supplement, BC bus pass.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/segments/tax-credits-deductions-persons-disabilities/disability-tax-credit.html
- **CDB (Canada Disability Benefit)** — Launched; first payments July 2025.
  Ages 18–64, must have DTC, must have filed taxes. Max $200/mo (2025-26);
  $204.20/mo from July 2026 (~$2,450/yr). Full if individual income (after a
  $10,000 working-income exemption) under ~$23,000; couples ~$32,500. Reduces
  20c per $1 above.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/disability/canada-disability-benefit.html
- **CCB** — Jul 2026–Jun 2027: max $8,157/yr (<6), $6,883/yr (6–17). Full if
  AFNI < $38,237. Child Disability Benefit add-on up to $3,411/yr (needs DTC).
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit-overview.html
- **CCC (Canada Caregiver Credit)** — 2025 up to $8,601 for an infirm adult
  dependant (line 30450/30425); reduced by dependant net income over $20,197.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/canada-caregiver-amount.html
- **CGEB (Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit — formerly GST/HST credit)** —
  Rename effective 2025 tax year. Jul 2025–Jun 2026: max $533 single, $698
  couple, $184/child. Phase-out from $45,521 AFNI at 5%.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/goods-services-tax-harmonized-sales-tax-gst-hst-credit.html
- **CDCP** — All ages eligible as of May 29 2025. Needs: no access to dental
  insurance, AFNI < $90,000, resident, filed taxes. Lower income → higher
  coverage.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/dental/dental-care-plan.html
- **CPP-D** — 2025 max $1,673.24/mo (2026 max $1,741.20); avg new ~$1,192/mo.
  Needs enough recent CPP contributions + severe and prolonged disability.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp-disability-benefit.html
- **CPP retirement** — 2025 max at 65 $1,433/mo (2026 $1,507.65); avg new
  ~$900/mo. From age 60 (reduced) to 70 (increased).
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp.html
- **OAS** — Jul–Sep 2026: 65–74 max $751.97/mo; 75+ max $827.17/mo. Age 65+,
  10+ yrs residency. Clawback starts ~$90,997 (2025).
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security.html
- **GIS** — Single max ~$1,108.74/mo; income < ~$22,488 (single). Automatic-ish
  once on OAS and filing taxes. Needs OAS.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement.html
- **EI (regular + sickness)** — 2026 max insurable $68,900; 55% rate; max
  $729/wk. Regular 14–45 wks; sickness up to 26 wks. Needs 420–700 insurable
  hours in qualifying period.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-regular-benefit.html
- **CWB** — 2025 basic max $1,633 single / $2,813 family; disability supplement
  $843. Single phase-out from $26,855 (zero at $37,742); family from $30,639
  (zero at $49,393). Refundable.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-45300-canada-workers-benefit-cwb.html
- **RDSP** — Grant up to $3,500/yr ($70k lifetime), match 300/200/100% by income
  (threshold $114,750, 2025). Bond up to $1,000/yr ($20k lifetime) if AFNI ≤
  $37,487 (partial to $57,375), no contribution needed. Needs DTC, under 60.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/disability/savings.html
- **Medical Expense Tax Credit** — Claim expenses above lesser of 3% of net
  income or ~$2,834 (2025). Non-refundable, 15% federal + provincial.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/lines-33099-33199-eligible-medical-expenses-you-claim-on-your-tax-return.html
- **Eligible Dependant Amount (line 30400)** — 2025 up to $16,129; +$2,687 if
  infirm; reduced by dependant net income. For single supporting a dependant.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-30400-amount-eligible-dependant.html
- **Canada Carbon Rebate — DISCONTINUED.** Fuel charge removed April 1 2025;
  final payment April 2025. Flag as ended; never returns eligible.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-carbon-rebate.html

## British Columbia

- **PWD (disability assistance)** — Single $1,483.50/mo (support + up to $500
  shelter), Dec 2025. Transportation supplement $52/mo. Needs PWD designation.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/services-for-people-with-disabilities/disability-assistance
- **BC Income Assistance** — Single employable $1,060/mo ($560 support + up to
  $500 shelter). Earnings exemption $600/mo.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/income-assistance/on-assistance
- **Fair PharmaCare** — Income-based deductible; no age limit; register with CRA
  income consent. Lower income → lower/zero deductible. Default deductible
  $10,000 if not registered.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/pharmacare-for-bc-residents/who-we-cover/fair-pharmacare-plan
- **MSP Supplementary Benefits** — Partial coverage for acupuncture,
  chiropractic, massage, naturopathy, physio, podiatry for MSP enrollees who
  qualify by income (Supplementary Benefits). Income-tested.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/benefits/services-covered-by-msp/supplementary-benefits
- **SAFER** — Age 60+, lived in BC 12 mo, rent > 30% of gross income, household
  income ≤ $40,000/yr (expanding toward $60k). Rent ceiling $1,150. Min $50/mo.
  Not for those on income/disability assistance.
  https://www.bchousing.org/housing-assistance/rental-assistance-programs/SAFER
- **BC Housing Registry** — Register for subsidized/social housing waitlist.
  Income under Housing Income Limits. Not a cash payment.
  https://www.bchousing.org/housing-assistance/rental-housing/subsidized-housing
- **RAP (Rental Assistance Program)** — Working families, income ≤ $60,000, ≥1
  dependent child, < $100k assets, filed taxes, rent > 30% income, in BC 12 mo,
  some employment income. Expanded April 2025.
  https://www.bchousing.org/housing-assistance/rental-assistance-programs/RAP
- **BC Seniors Supplement** — Automatic top-up for OAS+GIS recipients; up to
  ~$99.30/mo single (no application). Confirms with GIS.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/seniors/financial-legal-matters/income-security-programs/seniors-supplement
- **BC Bus Pass** — $45/yr. PWD recipients, or low-income seniors (OAS+GIS, or
  60+ on disability assistance; income under ~$24,000).
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/buses-taxis-limos/bus-pass
- **BC Home Owner Grant** — Basic $570 (Metro/CRD/FVRD) / $770 (elsewhere);
  seniors/disabled/veterans up to $845 / $1,045. 2026 threshold $2.075M,
  reduces $5 per $1,000 over. Principal residence.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **WorkBC Assistive Technology Services** — Funds assistive tech / workplace
  accommodations for people with disabilities to get or keep work.
  https://www.workbc.ca/employment-services/assistive-technology-services
- **BC Family Benefit** — 2025: max $1,750 first child, $1,100 second, $900
  each additional. Full if AFNI < $30,176; reduced to $96,562. Lone-parent
  supplement up to $500/yr. Bonus ended July 2025.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/affordability/family-benefit
- **BC Climate Action Tax Credit — DISCONTINUED.** Carbon tax repealed April 1
  2025; final credit payment April 2025. Flag as ended.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/personal/credits/climate-action
